2. Hosting and content delivery network (Cloudflare)

This website is delivered through Cloudflare, Inc., 101 Townsend St., San Francisco, CA 94107, USA (“Cloudflare”). When you open the site, Cloudflare processes connection data, namely your IP address, the date and time of the request, the requested URL, your browser’s user agent and, where applicable, the referrer. This data is needed to deliver and secure the website.

From this connection data Cloudflare also creates aggregated traffic statistics on its servers (for example the number of requests), which the operator can view in the Cloudflare dashboard. No script is embedded in this website for this purpose, no cookies are set and your device is not accessed.

The legal basis is Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR. The legitimate interest is a secure and reliable delivery of the website.

Data may be transferred to the USA in the process. The transfer relies on the European Commission’s adequacy decision on the EU-US Data Privacy Framework (Art. 45 GDPR); Cloudflare’s Data Processing Addendum applies in addition. Further information: Cloudflare’s privacy policy.

The storage period follows Cloudflare’s retention periods. The operator of this website does not store these log data itself.

3. Processing of your files in the browser

Model files (STL, OBJ, 3MF) and project files that you load into the tool are processed exclusively locally in your browser. They are not transmitted to the operator or to third parties. The files that are produced (DXF, SVG, PNG, JPG, ZIP) are also created in your browser and downloaded from there to your device.

If you click a link to another website (for example to the source code on GitHub), you leave this website; the respective provider is responsible for the data processing there.

4. Storage on your device

The application stores settings in your browser’s storage (localStorage and IndexedDB). This data stays on your device and is not transmitted to the operator or to third parties. It contains no identifiers, is not used to recognise you or to measure reach, and is not used for fingerprinting. The website itself sets no cookies and uses neither sessionStorage nor the Cache API nor a service worker.

5. Cloudflare security cookies

When you open the website, Cloudflare may set the following cookies for security reasons:

  • __cf_bm: part of bot detection (Bot Management or Bot Fight Mode). The cookie helps to tell humans from automated access. Lifetime: 30 minutes.
  • cf_clearance: stores that you have passed a security check (challenge) so that it is not repeated on every visit. The lifetime is set by the website’s configuration; it is usually between about 30 minutes and a few hours.

These cookies are not used for advertising or reach measurement. The legal bases are § 25(2) no. 2 TDDDG (strictly necessary for the requested use of a secure website) and Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR (legitimate interest in security and protection against abuse).

6. Contact by email

If you contact us by email, we process your details (email address, content of the message) only to deal with your request. The legal basis is Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR (legitimate interest in answering enquiries) and, for enquiries related to a contract, Art. 6(1)(b) GDPR. We delete the data as soon as it is no longer needed to deal with the request and no statutory retention obligations apply.

7. No analytics or tracking tools

No analytics or tracking tools are embedded in this website. There is no advertising, no profiling and no automated decision-making, including profiling, within the meaning of Art. 22 GDPR. The aggregated statistics described in section 2 are created by Cloudflare on its servers from the connection data.

8. Your rights

You have the following rights against the controller:

  • Access (Art. 15 GDPR)
  • Rectification (Art. 16 GDPR)
  • Erasure (Art. 17 GDPR)
  • Restriction of processing (Art. 18 GDPR)
  • Data portability (Art. 20 GDPR)
  • Objection to processing (Art. 21 GDPR)

Right to object: Where we process data on the basis of legitimate interests (Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR), you may object to this processing at any time on grounds relating to your particular situation.

You also have the right to lodge a complaint with a data protection supervisory authority (Art. 77 GDPR), for example with the authority at your place of habitual residence or place of work.

To exercise your rights, write to info@laserslicer.com.

9. Necessity of providing data

Providing personal data is required neither by law nor by contract. Without the connection data described in section 2, however, the website cannot technically be delivered.
